Operations Coordinator
Columbus Youth Arts Alliance, Columbus, OH

Columbus Youth Arts Alliance is a nonprofit running after-school and
summer arts programs at 12 sites for 2,000 students a year.

What you will do
- Order supplies and keep inventory across all 12 sites.
- Track the program budget.
- Schedule staff and spaces.
- Keep program leads aligned through regular check-ins.
- Work with vendors, site partners and our finance manager.

What we need
- 3+ years coordinating programs, budgets or logistics.
- Strong spreadsheet skills.
- Clear communication.
- Schools or youth programs a plus.
