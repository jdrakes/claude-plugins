Lakeside Freight: Staff Backend Engineer

Team: Shipment Platform
Location: Remote (United States)
Compensation: $190,000 to $225,000 base

An invented posting for the cover-letter eval. The company, the role and
every detail below are made up.

About the role

Lakeside Freight moves loads for about four hundred regional carriers. Every
carrier integration, the dispatch board and the billing system read from one
shipment-tracking database. This year we are splitting that database into
separate services, and no carrier can be asked to switch on the same day as
every other. You will lead that migration and own the services that come out
of it.

What you will do

- Lead the split of the shipment-tracking database into services, with a
  migration path that lets each carrier integration move on its own schedule.
- Own the correctness of the invoicing data that carriers and our finance
  team reconcile against, including how a corrected invoice is recorded.
- Design service APIs that stay correct when a carrier's system is slow,
  retries, or goes down.
- Run PostgreSQL at scale: schema changes, backfills, and query performance
  on tables that never stop taking writes.
- Instrument the services so the on-call engineer can see what failed and
  why without paging the author.
- Mentor engineers on the team and write design notes others can act on.

What we are looking for

- Eight or more years building backend services that other teams depend on.
- A migration you have led where callers moved without a coordinated release.
- Comfort owning a service end to end, from the schema to the dashboard.
