# Standing desk: home office

**Constraint.** Back pain after long sitting days, and the only free wall is 120 cm wide, so the desk must fit 120 cm and switch height easily enough to be used several times a day.
**Decision.** Fernwood Lift 2, $480. Runner-up: Calder Rise, $620, lost because its narrowest top is 140 cm and the wall is 120 cm.

## Options
| Option | Price | 3-year cost | Ruled out because |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fernwood Lift 2 | $480 | $480 | Chosen: fits 120 cm, dual motor, 5-year motor warranty |
| Calder Rise | $620 | $620 | Narrowest top is 140 cm; does not fit the wall |
| Generic crank desk | $210 | $210 | A hand crank is too slow to switch often, so it would stay at sitting height |
| Converter on the current desk | $160 | $160 | Raises only the keyboard and screen; the chair still blocks standing room |

## Reasoning
The constraint is standing often enough to help the back, inside 120 cm. Only the two motorised desks make switching easy enough to happen several times a day, and only the Fernwood fits the wall. Its 5-year motor warranty sets the reassess date.
