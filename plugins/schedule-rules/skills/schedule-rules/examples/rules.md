# Rules: Dana Whitlock

An invented person, to show the shape of a rules file. Times are
America/Denver. Every weekday unless stated.

## Blocks

- Swim: Mon, Wed and Fri, 7:00 to 8:00.
- Studio hours: Tue and Thu, 9:00 to 12:00, for writing.
- Team sync: Mon, Wed and Fri, 9:30 to 9:45, on the work calendar.
- Physio: Mon 13:00 to 13:45, a weekly placeholder. The clinic sends a
  real invite per session; where one exists, skip that week's placeholder.

## Protected blocks

Never offered for interview or call times: Swim and Studio hours.

## Hard boundaries

- School run: never moved. Its time is the Household calendar's "School
  run" event; read it live.
- Evenings from 17:30: no calls or meetings.

## Cancel on out of office

Swim and the Physio placeholder. A booked Physio session is kept.

## Contingencies

- Child home sick: flag that Swim should drop.
- School closed for snow: Dana takes the day off rather than compress the
  schedule; flag it.
- Out of office: one all-day out-of-office event per day. Cancel that
  day's blocks under Cancel on out of office; keep medical appointments.
  The School run and evenings are untouched.
- Travel day: no calls before 10:00 at the destination.

## Minimum buffer

10 minutes between blocks. A shorter handoff is flagged.

## Cadences

- Swim: 3 times a week.
- Studio hours: 2 mornings a week.
- Physio: weekly until 2027-03-31, then stop.

## Conflict priority

- Work calendar against a personal block: the work calendar wins.
- A medical appointment against Swim: Swim gives way.
- A low-priority one-off over a protected block: flag, do not resolve.
- A hard appointment against the School run: the appointment wins; flag
  that the run needs cover.

## Calendars

- Personal: read and write.
- Household: read only; the School run lives there.
- Work: read only, busy or free only. Never create, edit or delete on it.
