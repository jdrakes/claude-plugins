# Outline: The Low Water Log

## Premise

Dora Penhallow pilots the chain ferry at Saltney, the last crossing on
the tidal river Cale before it widens into the estuary. She wants to keep
the crossing licence her late father held for thirty years. She needs to
stop covering for other people's mistakes and say plainly what she saw.
The antagonist is Victor Lusk, the harbour master, who has altered the
ferry's crossing log to put a night crossing she never made under her
name.

## Acts

- Act one: chapters 1 to 2.
- Act two: chapter 3.

## Beats

1. Dora runs the first crossing of the day and finds an entry in the log,
   in a hand that is not hers, for a crossing on Tuesday night.
2. Dora takes the log to Lusk at the harbour office. He tells her the log
   is the log, and that a pilot who disputes her own entries will not
   keep a licence.
3. At low water Dora checks the logged time against the tide table and
   finds the river was too shallow for the ferry to cross. She decides to
   take the log to the river inspector instead of to Lusk.

## Threads

- The altered log: found in chapter 1, disputed in chapter 2, disproved
  by the tide table in chapter 3.
